## 3. Constraint

Describe the failure mode or trade-off. Do not insert numerical performance claims without a primary benchmark whose hardware, software version, workload, and method are stated.

## 4. Evidence

Tie each externally verifiable claim to a primary source: an official specification, source permalink, vendor architecture manual, or original paper. Distinguish measurements you reproduced from measurements reported by a source.

## 5. Decision

Turn the mechanism into a bounded decision rule. State when the architecture applies, when it does not, and what must be measured locally.
